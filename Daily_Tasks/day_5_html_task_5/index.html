🔎 Destination Information

Goa Travel Guide

Goa is an excellent destination for beach lovers and travelers looking for a relaxing vacation.

  • Popular Beaches: Baga, Calangute, Anjuna
  • Famous Food: Fish Curry, Vindaloo
  • Average Trip: 3-5 Days

Kerala Travel Guide

Kerala is known for its natural beauty, backwaters, Ayurveda and peaceful atmosphere.

  • Popular Places: Munnar, Alleppey, Kochi
  • Famous Food: Appam, Kerala Sadya
  • Average Trip: 4-7 Days

Rajasthan Travel Guide

Rajasthan offers magnificent forts, royal palaces, desert adventures and traditional culture.

  • Popular Places: Jaipur, Jaisalmer, Udaipur
  • Famous Food: Dal Baati Churma
  • Average Trip: 5-8 Days
